RESEARCH / QUESTIONS & METHODS

What I study.
How I approach it.

My research spans representation learning, interpretable AI, and scientific applications. These themes connect the projects and publications below.

01

Foundation models for biological sequences

Developing transformer-based models for sequence understanding and genomic classification across diverse organisms. I build reproducible pipelines that combine biological datasets, metadata, and scientific knowledge.

Methods: Python, PyTorch, Transformers, CNNs, BiLSTMs, and GRUs; model development and benchmarking.

02

Antimicrobial peptide discovery

Exploring machine learning and deep learning for antimicrobial peptide discovery through ALERT: ADML — Antimicrobial Learning Exploration & Research for Targets.

Output: A 2026 manuscript under review at Computers in Biology and Medicine.

03

Interpretability and reasoning in language models

Investigating LLM reasoning and representation behavior through hidden-state analysis, with a focus on internal model dynamics and interpretability.

Output: Measuring Deep Thought in LLMs Through Hidden State Analysis, ICPRAI 2026.

04

Multimodal learning and precision psychiatry

Studying graph-based metabolite networks and multimodal biomedical data at the intersection of AI and computational neuroscience, including research on major depressive disorder and neuropsychiatric disorders.

Methods: Graph neural networks, multimodal learning, and interpretable, reproducible Python analysis pipelines. Related 2026 manuscripts are in preparation.

05

Quantum learning and molecular property prediction

Investigating the integration of quantum representation learning with graph intelligence for neurodegenerative drug discovery. My undergraduate thesis explored cancer diagnosis and biomarker prediction using quantum machine learning.

Status: Molecular property prediction manuscript in preparation (2026).
